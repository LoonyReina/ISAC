## What the lesson computes

The accepted core computes all N² ordered Eq. (5) contributions, including
same-station and reciprocal pairs, then evaluates the realized information
matrix before Eq. (6) averaging. The browser reads retained input coordinates;
plots and metrics are recalculated, never substituted with saved answers.
The normalized teaching setting has q=1 and beta=2. Adding stations retains the
per-station scale and increases participating resources. There is no physical
metre/watt calibration or fixed-total-power claim.

Finite, certified-singular, unstable, unresolved, invalid and incomplete
attempts remain visible. Cancellation does not publish a completed aggregate.
The ellipse is an information-bound contour with no probability coverage
interpretation. Inverse trace and root summaries are not receiver RMSE.
The N=2 population inverse-trace mean diverges under independent uniform
bearings and positive finite radii; finite samples do not prove convergence.
No N≥3 integrability or population-convergence claim is made.
