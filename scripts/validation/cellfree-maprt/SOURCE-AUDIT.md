## Important distinction from a marginalized likelihood ratio

The MAPRT numerator/denominator are optimized joint densities, not integrated densities. The present implementation explicitly computes their optimizer and cost and independently verifies direct Gaussian log densities. For this fixed linear proper-Gaussian model, elimination of quadratic nuisance yields the same data-dependent quadratic form as the integrated LRT; their constants differ. The MAPRT uses log C3; the integrated LRT uses log det(Sigma0) − log det(Sigma1). Thus independently recalibrated fixed-design rankings can agree without the tests having the same definition. This special equivalence does not make the former two-station teaching toy the paper algorithm, and it is not a distribution-free CFAR theorem. Parameter-dependent or hypothesis-varying designs and nuisance priors require care.

## Source numerical configuration and unrecovered choices

The source §IX/Table III uses 500 m × 500 m, a central 15 m × 15 m hotspot, 16 transmit APs, 2 nearest receive APs, 8 UEs, tau_c=200, tau_p=10, default tau_s=10, Ptx=1 W, pilot power=0.2 W, fc=1.9 GHz, bandwidth=20 MHz, noise variance −94 dBm, UE SINR target 3 dB, default clutter scaling s=0.3, and Pfa=0.1 or 0.01. RCS coefficients are independent unit-variance Swerling-I, constant over a sensing block; physical RCS variances are equal across links. General correlated R_rcs is allowed in the derivation but is not the numerical section's RCS setting.

Communication and clutter path loss reference 3GPP Urban Microcell [49]; communication spatial correlation references the local-scattering model [24 §2.5.3]. Source expectations average 1000 channel realizations and 1000 hotspot target locations; reported Pd averages 100 UE-location/channel setups. It describes 100/Pfa RCS and (100/Pfa)·tau_s noise realizations per setup, but does not fully specify pairing/reuse or a separate threshold-calibration budget. We do not silently reinterpret those as independently calibrated Bernoulli trials.

An exhaustive text inspection of fixed v2 did not locate a numerical value for M, a RZF regularizer value, numerical covariance angular spreads, the AP coordinate list/seed, symbol distribution, channel-estimation pilot reuse assignment, optimizer tolerances or an explicit threshold-estimation protocol. Figure 2 supplies a plotted AP geometry, not a numerical dataset. These omissions prevent a verified exact source-curve match. No plot digitization or inferred settings are presented as the original simulation.

## Selected experiment boundary

This package targets a reduced-dimensional, conditional implementation of the actual paper model and algorithms, not Fig. 3–7 or a full-paper reproduction. Four transmit APs, two receive APs, M=2 antennas and two UEs preserve nontrivial multi-static vector observations, unknown RCS and clutter, imperfect communication CSI, per-AP constraints, and common stream powers. The target is fixed; channel covariances and geometry are explicitly supplied. Published fc/noise/power/SINR/tau_s/Pfa values are reused where stated. Synthetic communication/clutter covariance choices replace the unrecovered full 3GPP/local-scattering deployment and must remain labeled as such. Detector tests also cover correlated RCS as a mathematical verification case, not the paper's Table III setting.

All paired detector comparisons use identical y, actual design, noise, alpha and h; allocation comparisons reuse the same underlying standard-normal seeds and symbols but necessarily have different x and y because powers differ. Per-AP budget is equal across allocations; actual used power/energy is reported separately. The comm-centric used energy is not equal by default. Truth is generation/evaluation-only. Independent calibration and evaluation seeds prevent reusing null observations to both fit and assess thresholds. Finite Monte Carlo intervals are conditional estimates with exact binomial intervals for each fixed threshold; calibration-order uncertainty is recorded separately. No all-location, all-SNR or full-calibration guarantee is claimed.
